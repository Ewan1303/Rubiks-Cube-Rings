Rubik's Cube Rings

Each of the cube's nine layers is drawn as a circle, and each sticker is a dot where its two layers cross. Turn a ring and the solid cube turns with it.

Moves

Mouse and touch

Drag a dot along its ring, or a square across the cube, to turn that layer. Click or tap a ring (or its letter) to turn it, and a dot or square to turn its face. Right-click or Shift-click turns the other way. Hover over a ring to see what it moves. An inner or outer ring also spins the face on that side of the cube, so that face's nine dots light up too, even though they sit away from the ring.

Solver

Solve spends a couple of seconds hunting for the shortest solution it can find, usually 19 or 20 moves for a well-scrambled cube (no position needs more than 20), then plays it one move at a time. Pause it to step through by hand. A half turn such as R2 counts as one move.
